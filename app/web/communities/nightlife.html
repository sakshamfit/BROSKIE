Community type · Club Night

Find your nightlife crew on +one

The in-app category is called Club Night, and it exists for one very specific problem: the 34-message “where are we going Saturday?” that ends with nobody going anywhere. A nightlife community is one persistent group for the going-out crowd — venue decided by poll, headcount visible, logistics said once.

“Rooftop first or straight to the dance floor?” — the bars fill as the votes land, and the poll lives in the group chat with the aftermovie, the cab-share voices and the “I'm leaving in ten” messages. If the plan changes at 11 pm, everybody in the community sees it at 11:00:01, because this is a real-time messenger.

Nightlife groups usually run invite-only or ask-to-join, and the admin controls are real: promote someone to organise a night off you, remove the guy who brings plus-ones, and know the last admin can't be locked out because the app won't let them. Open groups exist too, for city-wide “find a crew tonight” communities — the live member count tells you which is which before you tap join.

What a club night looks like inside +one

  • You want a regular going-out crew, not a fresh group chat every Saturday
  • You plan nights out for people and need RSVPs and a headcount before booking the table
  • You're new in town and want to find the group that's already out there

Built with the group tools that ship in the app

  • Live venue and timing polls with results filling as votes land
  • Invite-only or ask-to-join doors — no uninvited plus-ones
  • Admins rename, promote and remove; the last admin never vanishes
  • Voice notes and late-night photos stay in one shared group
