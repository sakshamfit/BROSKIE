Community type · Game Night

Find a game night squad on +one

The Game Night category covers the whole spectrum the app actually ships for: squad nights, board-game tables, 5-a-side on Sunday. What they all need is the same thing — reliably gathering exactly the right number of people at exactly one time — so a gaming community is basically a standing plan that lives in a group chat.

“Tonight, 9 pm — what are we running?” goes up as a poll; the answer isn't a wall of replies, it's two bars that fill while everyone's in call-or-no-call arguments. Custom-room links, set times and the “one more round” decisions all land in one place. And when it's game time, the group call is already there — voice with every member, no switching apps mid-match.

Most squads run invite-only with rotating invite codes — admins can revoke and regenerate +one/c/… links, so the room stays the room. Open gaming communities work the other way: public cards on Discover with live member counts, made for finding the fifth player who isn't your friend yet.

What a game night looks like inside +one

  • You play a squad game and you're tired of “sorry, can't tonight — need one more”
  • You host the Friday board-game night and need RSVPs, headcount and the playlist in one place
  • You want a Sunday football/cricket group where the pitch and time get settled by the group, not one organiser

Built with the group tools that ship in the app

  • Live polls for which game, whose place, and what time
  • Group voice calls with every member, started from the chat
  • Invite links admins can revoke and rotate — the lobby stays yours
  • Media and voice notes in the group for replays and brackets
