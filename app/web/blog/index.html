How to Find a Running Partner

Most people don't need another fitness app — they need one person who shows up at 6 am. Here's the playbook on Plus One: post on The Network with #running, join or start a Running Group community, set it to open joining, and put the weekly route in the pinned poll. Discover communities based on shared interests, whether you want a study group at your university or a running crew near campus.
