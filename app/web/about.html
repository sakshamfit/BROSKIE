About Plus One

Plus One is a community-based social platform built to help people connect, chat, share posts and discover communities around shared interests. Whether you are looking for a running partner, a study group, or colleagues at work, Plus One helps you find the people worth talking to.

Built with Expo, React Native, Node.js, Socket.IO, and a durable SQLite backend. Open source at github.com/sakshamfit/BROSKIE.
