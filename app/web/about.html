Why +one exists

A social platform that feels like paper

Plus One is a community-based social platform built to help people connect, communicate and find communities around shared interests — whether that's a running partner, a study group, or the people at your workplace who actually reply.

Open the app Read the repo

Interests first, follower counts never

Most networks optimise for who you already know. Plus One starts from what you care about: a tag, a community, a run, a chai. Follows turn into colleagues, colleagues turn into chats — the feed is a place to meet, not a place to perform.

Drawn, not designed into oblivion

The interface is "Graphite & Pulp": ink strokes on warm off-white paper, a faint drafting grid, hand-drawn boxes, masking-tape chips, and one highlighter-yellow accent used the way a real marker is — sparingly. Headlines are set in Bricolage Grotesque, body text in Karla, labels in JetBrains Mono. This website wears the exact same tokens as the app.

Privacy is a feature, not a policy page

  • Message requests — unknown senders wait for your approval
  • Disappearing messages hard-deleted from the server
  • Block, mute, status privacy, per-post audiences
  • Password-confirmed account deletion with safe cleanup
  • No fake seed data — every account is a real sign-up

How it's built

One codebase, three surfaces: Expo / React Native on iOS, Android and web, Node.js + Socket.IO on the backend, SQLite on a persistent volume with automatic backups every six hours.

  • Real-time messaging, presence, calls (WebRTC)
  • Installable PWA on the web
  • 100% SVG icons and 1445 Twemoji everywhere
  • Open source: github.com/sakshamfit/BROSKIE

The short version

Plus One ("+one") launched as an experiment: what if a messenger looked like a sketchbook and worked like a serious product? It now ships realtime chat, a worldwide feed, communities, stories, polls, calls, voice notes and a daily AI greeting — on iOS, Android and the web, for free.

The name is the point: everyone you meet here is a potential +1 — the running partner, the study buddy, the colleague who becomes a friend.
