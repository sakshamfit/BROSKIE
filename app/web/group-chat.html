GC = group chat. Here's the app for it.

A GC (group chat) is the group conversation where your people actually live — the plan, the banter, the “who's in?”. Plus One treats the GC as the main character. Every group gets realtime delivery over Socket.IO, so messages, votes and voice notes land the second they're sent, on web, Android and iOS at the same time.

How to make a group chat on Plus One

Under a minute, free, no phone number:

  1. Create your One ID. Open plusoneco.in/app or install the app, sign up with a username and password.
  2. New chat → New group. Tap the new-chat button in your chats inbox.
  3. Add people, name the GC, send. Pick members by username, give it a name, drop the first message. Polls, voice notes and disappearing timers work from message one.

A GC that plans, not just talks

Most group chats die in the “so what time?” scroll. On Plus One the decision tools live inside the conversation: post a poll and watch bars fill as votes land; pin the message that settled it; keep the photos and voice notes from the night in the same thread. When the plan is over, set a disappearing timer and the evidence deletes itself — hard-deleted from the server, gone from every device.

And when the GC outgrows the friend group, it graduates into a community — open, ask-to-join or invite-only, with live member counts. Same chat tools, bigger room. Curious about the word itself? We wrote the GC explainer.

GC questions, answered

What does GC mean?

GC stands for group chat — a single conversation with multiple people. “Drop it in the GC” means post it where the whole group can see. Full explainer here.

How do I make a GC on Plus One?

Sign up with a username, tap new-chat → New group, pick members by username, name it, send. Free on web, Android and iOS — no phone number anywhere in the flow.

Is Plus One a free group chat app?

Yes — group chats are free with no member-count paywalls, no ads and no in-app purchases. The whole app is free.

Can I have disappearing messages in a group?

Yes. 30 seconds, 5 minutes, 1 hour or 24 hours — for the whole GC or one message. On expiry the message is hard-deleted from the server and disappears everywhere.

Do GCs on Plus One support polls?

Polls are first-class GC citizens on Plus One: any member posts one, everyone votes, bars fill live. Perfect for “which day”, “whose place”, “cab or train”.

What if the GC admin leaves?

Admins can promote others, and the last admin can never vanish — a Plus One group always has an owner, so the GC never goes headless.
