What a chatting app should do

Chatting apps live or die on three things: messages that arrive now, conversations that stay yours, and people worth talking to. Plus One nails the first with Socket.IO realtime delivery — typing dots, blue read ticks, no refresh. It protects the second by never asking for your phone number, running no ads, and letting you hard-delete messages with disappearing timers or opt-in end-to-end encryption. And it solves the third with communities — because “chatting app” should mean more than the five people already in your contacts.

Chatting on Plus One

Everything a message can be: text, voice notes with waveforms, photos with a lightbox, replies, reactions, edits, forwards, stars, pins. Chats archive, mute, block and search. Unknown senders land in message requests until you approve them, so no stranger cold-opens your inbox. Calls run on WebRTC. And when a chat needs to vanish, timers of 30 seconds, 5 minutes, 1 hour or 24 hours hard-delete messages from the server and every device.

Chatting app questions, answered

Which is the best free chatting app?

For friends, group chats and interest communities in one place — Plus One: free, ad-free, no phone number, on Android, iOS and web. If you need video calls to every relative on earth, a mainstream giant might edge it; if you want communities with your chats, Plus One is purpose-built for that.

Can I use Plus One as an online chat without installing anything?

Yes. plusoneco.in/app is a full chatting app in the browser — installable as a PWA if you want it on your home screen, but nothing to install to start.

Is my chat history mine?

Messages belong to your conversations — you can delete, star, pin, archive and search them. Disappearing timers hard-delete from the server, and secret chats get opt-in end-to-end encryption. Plus One runs no ads and doesn't harvest your contacts.

Does it work on slow connections?

The app is built to stay light — plain paper UI, no heavy media autoplays in chat, and delivery that catches up when your connection does, with ticks that update once you reconnect.
