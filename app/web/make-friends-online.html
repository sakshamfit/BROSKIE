How to make friends online on Plus One

Making friends online works when there's something to talk about. That's why Plus One starts with communities instead of profiles — you meet people mid-plan, not mid-pitch.

  1. Create a username

    Open plusoneco.in/app or install the app and sign up with a username and password. No phone number, no photo required, under a minute.

  2. Join a community around something you do

    Browse communities — running, travel, gaming, study groups, chai chats, nightlife, house parties — and join one that's open, ask to join, or use a friend's invite link. Live member counts show you which groups are actually active. Or start your own and set the door.

  3. Talk in the group first

    Vote in the poll. Answer the “anyone free Saturday?” Share a voice note from the trail. Group chat is low-pressure: you get to know people through what you're doing together, not through an interview.

  4. Move to one-to-one when it clicks

    Send a direct message. If you're not contacts yet it lands in their Message Requests — and theirs land in yours — so nobody gets cold-opened and everyone stays in control. Add your college or workplace under Colleagues to find people from your own campus or office.

Three ways to meet people on Plus One

  • Communities (by interest): purpose-built groups with their own real-time chat, live polls and join policies — open, ask-to-join or invite-only. Find your people by what you do.
  • Colleagues (by place): add your college, institution, organisation or workplace; see who else is there, send a connection request, and open a chat the moment it's accepted. The Today strip shows who from your places is around or online right now.
  • The Network (by curiosity): a worldwide feed with tags and a Following lens. Like a post, comment, follow the author — “Sunday long run, anyone?” turns into a community with one tap.

Staying safe while making friends online

  • You control the door. First messages from outside your contacts wait in Message Requests. Accept, delete or block — opening a chat never sends a request; only sending a message does.
  • Nothing personal is required. No phone number, no contact upload, photo optional. Share more only when you want to.
  • Mute, block, report from any chat or profile. In-app reports go to a human-reviewed Safety Center; automated detection flags, it never punishes on its own.
  • Disappearing messages (30s / 5m / 1h / 24h) hard-delete from the server; secret chats add opt-in end-to-end encryption.
  • Offline rules still apply: meet new people in public, tell someone where you're going, and keep addresses out of the group chat until you know the group.

Making friends online, answered

How can I make friends online for free?

Join a community built around something you already do, talk in the group first, then move to one-to-one chats. Plus One is free — no ads, no swiping, no photo or phone number required — on web, Android and iOS.

Is Plus One a dating app?

No. It's a chatting app for friends, group chats and interest-based communities. No swiping, no matching.

Do I need a photo or a phone number?

No. Username and password only. A photo is optional and your phone number is never asked for.

Is it safe to make friends online here?

Message Requests stop anyone cold-opening your inbox; mute, block and report are one tap away; disappearing messages hard-delete from the server; secret chats are end-to-end encrypted. Reports are reviewed by people.

Can I find friends near me or from my college?

Yes — add your college, organisation or workplace and connect with colleagues from the same place; the Today strip shows who's around right now. Communities show live member counts so you can find active groups in your city.

Can I make friends online without random strangers?

That's exactly the design. You meet people inside a community you chose, around something you both care about — and nobody can DM you until you accept their request.
